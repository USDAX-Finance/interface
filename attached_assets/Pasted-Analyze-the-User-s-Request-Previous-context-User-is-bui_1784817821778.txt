## 1. Introduction

**ArchonX** is a decentralized dual-token protocol built on the Robinhood Chain. It aims to provide a stable, overcollateralized digital dollar (`USDAX`) backed by crypto assets (WETH/WBTC) while rewarding long-term participants through a governance token (`AKX`) with a variable staking APY.

ArchonX is designed with three core principles:
- **Stability**: USDAX maintains a 1:1 peg with the USD through robust overcollateralization (150% ratio).
- **Efficiency**: Low gas fees and fast finality leveraging the Arbitrum Orbit stack of Robinhood Chain.
- **Community Governance**: AKX holders govern the protocol parameters (APY, collateral types, fees).

---

## 2. Protocol Overview

The protocol consists of five primary smart contracts working in harmony:

| Contract | Purpose |
| :--- | :--- |
| `USDAX.sol` | The ERC-20 stablecoin. Pegged to $1. Mints/burns only by DSCEngine. |
| `AKX.sol` | The ERC-20 governance token with voting (ERC20Votes). Max supply 100M. |
| `DSCEngine.sol` | The core engine. Handles collateral deposits, debt tracking, health factors, and liquidations. |
| `OracleLib.sol` | Abstracts Chainlink price feeds with a 3-hour stale price timeout. |
| `Staking.sol` | Manages AKX staking, calculates variable APY, and enforces a 7-day cooldown. |

4.2 Oracle & Price Feeds
ArchonX uses Chainlink Price Feeds to determine the USD value of collateral assets.

Stale Price Protection: Any price older than 3 hours is rejected.

Decimals Normalization: Chainlink returns 8 decimals (e.g., $2,000.00000000). The OracleLib converts this to 18 decimals (2,000 * 10^18) for internal math consistency.

5. Core Mechanics
5.1 Overcollateralization & Minting
To mint USDAX, a user must deposit a collateral asset (e.g., WETH). The protocol enforces a 150% collateralization ratio (equivalent to an 80% Loan-to-Value, LTV).

Formula:

math
Max Mintable USDAX = (Deposited Collateral USD Value) / 1.5
Example:

User deposits $150 worth of WETH.

Chainlink price = $150.

User can mint up to $150 / 1.5 = $100 USDAX.

5.2 Health Factor
The Health Factor (HF) determines if a position is at risk of liquidation.

math
Health Factor = (Total Collateral Value (USD) * 1e18) / (Minted USDAX Amount (USD) * 1e18)
Health Factor	Status
∞ (Infinite)	User has no debt.
> 1.0	Safe. Position is overcollateralized.
== 1.0	Threshold. Position is exactly at the minimum collateralization.
< 1.0	⚠️ Under-Collateralized. Eligible for immediate liquidation.
5.3 Liquidation Mechanism
If a user's Health Factor drops below 1.0, anyone can call the liquidate() function to repay the user's debt in exchange for their collateral plus a 10% bonus.

Liquidation Example:

User A: $90 collateral, $100 debt. (HF = 0.9)

Liquidator (User B) calls liquidate(User A, $100 debt).

User B pays $100 worth of USDAX (burned).

User B receives User A's $100 collateral + 10% bonus = $110 collateral.

User B Profit: $10.

5.4 Staking & APY
Users can stake their AKX tokens to earn rewards paid in AKX.

Base APY: 15% annualized.

Variable APY: The actual APY is inversely proportional to the user's share of the total staking pool.

math
User APY = Base APY * (Total Staked AKX / User Staked AKX)
This incentivizes early/large stakers with higher yields.

Cooldown Period: 7 days. Users cannot unstake until 7 days have passed since their last stake deposit. This prevents bank runs and stabilizes the voting power.

6. User Guides
6.1 How to Mint USDAX
Navigate to the ArchonX DApp.

Connect your wallet (MetaMask/Phantom) to Robinhood Chain.

Select "Mint USDAX".

Choose your collateral asset (e.g., WETH).

Enter the amount of collateral to deposit.

The UI will display the max USDAX you can mint.

Enter the amount of USDAX to mint.

Click "Approve" for the collateral token, then "Confirm Deposit".

Wait for the transaction to finalize. USDAX will appear in your wallet.

6.2 How to Redeem Collateral
Go to the "Dashboard".

Click "Redeem".

Select the collateral asset and enter the amount to withdraw.

Enter the amount of USDAX to burn (must cover the collateral value).

Confirm the transaction. Collateral will be sent to your wallet.

6.3 How to Stake AKX
Go to the "Staking" page.

Enter the amount of AKX to stake.

Approve the AKX spending and confirm the stake transaction.

Your staking rewards start accumulating immediately.

6.4 How to Claim Staking Rewards
Visit the "Staking" page.

Click the "Claim Rewards" button.

Confirm the transaction. Rewards are sent directly to your wallet.

6.5 How to Liquidate an Underwater Position
Check the protocol dashboard for positions with Health Factor < 1.0.

Select the position and click "Liquidate".

Enter the debt amount you wish to cover.

Confirm the transaction. You will receive the liquidated collateral + 10% bonus instantly.

    address tokenCollateralAddress,
    uint256 amountCollateral,

Description: Burns USDAX to release deposited collateral.

Reverts If: Burn amount is insufficient for the collateral requested.

solidity
function liquidate(address user, address tokenCollateralAddress, uint256 debtToCover) external
Description: Repays a user's debt and claims their collateral + bonus.

Requirements: getHealthFactor(user) < 1e18.

solidity
function getHealthFactor(address user) public view returns (uint256)
Returns: The health factor scaled by 1e18.

solidity

Requirements: block.timestamp >= stake.timestamp + 7 days.

9. Security & Audits
9.1 Security Considerations

Stale Price Protection: Oracle prices are time-locked to prevent manipulation during volatile periods.

Overcollateralization: The 150% ratio acts as a buffer against high market volatility.

10. Glossary
Term	Definition
USDAX	The stablecoin native to ArchonX, pegged 1:1 to the US Dollar.
AKX	The governance and staking token of ArchonX.
Overcollateralized	A debt position where the collateral value exceeds the borrowed value (150% in ArchonX).
Health Factor	A numeric metric representing the safety of a loan position. Must be > 1.0.
Liquidation	The process of forcibly repaying a user's debt to secure their collateral when they are undercollateralized.
